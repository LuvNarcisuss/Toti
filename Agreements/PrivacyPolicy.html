1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how the current Toti iPhone app handles information in Lounge, Voice, Notebook, Chats and Me. "We" means the operator identified above. It distinguishes information sent to Toti's Supabase backend from device-only archives, operating-system services and information you choose to send outside the app.

Toti now provides online guest accounts and cloud storage for supported features. Calling an account anonymous does not mean that no personal information is processed. Account identifiers, content, session records and network information can relate to you. Private Notebook entries are cloud-hosted but are not exposed to other members through the normal app interface.

2. Information processed by feature

2.1 Guest account, profile and agreement records

Log in creates or reuses a Supabase guest authentication identity with a unique account identifier. The service processes session/access and refresh credentials, account status and authentication timestamps. Your display name, biography and chosen avatar information support your profile and authorship labels. Other members can see profile information when permitted by the app's access and blocking rules.

The current login does not ask for an email address, phone number, password, identity document or date of birth, and does not use Sign in with Apple. Supabase and its infrastructure still process network and security information for requests. An unlinked guest cannot currently recover access by email or move the account to another device through an account-linking flow.

Toti stores the agreement version and acceptance time with the account. The version identifies the configured PDF content hashes. The service records acceptance during enrollment or accepted login; it does not store proof that you read every page. Existing historical local acceptance records may remain in the separate archive.

2.2 Lounge posts, drafts and interactions

The backend stores post identifiers, author identifiers, text, topic, timestamps, version/status, an optional photo revision and an optional room reference. Comments, likes and saved choices associate your account with content. Published posts and comments can be shown to eligible signed-in members, subject to blocking and content status. A saved private draft is restricted to its owner.

Writing a draft or publishing a post uses different visibility states. A cloud-save acknowledgement means the server accepted that operation; it does not promise an audience, immediate display on every device or approval of a photo. Likes and saved choices are stored to maintain account state, not to build an advertising profile.

2.3 Private Notebook

Notebook records include note identifiers, title, lyrics/chords/ideas text, category, optional mood, pinned status and timestamps. They are stored under your account and used for editing, searching and organizing your work. Other members do not receive access to these notes through the app.

Turn into a post copies the selected note's text into a separate Lounge draft. It does not automatically publish the draft or delete the note. Deleting or editing either copy does not automatically change the other. Private access controls are not end-to-end encryption against authorized service administration.

2.4 Chats, groups and relationships

Toti stores conversation/group identifiers, participant and owner references, group names, invitations and their status, membership boundaries, message text, sender identifiers and timestamps. It also stores follow/block choices and conversation preferences such as drafts, pinning, muting, hiding and personal read markers.

Conversation content is available to authorized participants; group access depends on accepted invitations, membership and the history boundaries enforced by the service. A profile search or relationship action uses information available to your account, not your device address book. Personal read markers are not recipient read receipts. There is no push-notification service, and periodic refresh is not proof that someone is online or has read a message.

2.5 Voice rooms and editorial content

Room records can include creator, title, description, topic, capacity/seat settings, status and timestamps. Membership and room messages link account identifiers to rooms. Eligible members can see room information, participation and permitted text history. Voice-room features do not currently capture or transmit microphone audio or live video.

Toti's labeled editorial prompts, room covers and illustrative portraits are supplied materials, not actual members or evidence of attendance. Viewing a material-only room does not by itself create a membership or send a message.

2.6 Selected images and review

When you select a post photo or avatar through the iOS photo picker and confirm the relevant action, Toti prepares a resized JPEG and sends it to the authenticated media service. The service validates and re-encodes the image, stores an account-owned revision and records its identifier, purpose, dimensions, byte count, digest, timestamps, attachment reference and review status or feedback. It does not upload the entire Photos library.

Pending or rejected images are owner-only through normal service access. Authorized operators can inspect submitted images to review them. Approval is an actual review decision, not an automatic consequence of uploading. Approved images are returned only through authorized app requests and applicable content/blocking rules. The earlier approved avatar remains available during replacement review; superseded or unattached revisions may remain in storage until cleanup or account deletion.

The original item in Photos is not altered or deleted. The upload pipeline works with a processed JPEG rather than intentionally transmitting the original full photo file. Information visible within an image, such as a face or written address, can still identify someone. Review what is visible before uploading.

2.7 Reports and safety records

Submitting a report sends the target identifier/type, selected reason, details and the reporting account context to the backend. The service records receipt and timestamps. Blocks are account relationships used to restrict supported discovery and interaction. These are online records; a successful submission is not a statement that staff have reviewed it. No review deadline or continuously staffed moderation service is represented as available.

2.8 Wallet and historical purchase records

Online purchases, gifts, balances and activity are currently unavailable. Disabled package cards and reference prices do not create a payment, a balance or a recipient earning. The online account does not import historical device-local Diamond credits or transaction records.

An earlier local app version may have retained balance, transaction identifiers and gift activity in its own device storage. Those records remain separate from the online service. Apple retains any purchases it previously processed under its own practices. Toti does not receive full card details or an Apple Account password through the app.

3. Device storage, permissions and the Local archive

The app stores the guest session in iOS Keychain for reuse on this device; this implementation does not synchronize it through iCloud Keychain. Account-specific pending-operation records can contain identifiers, request text, selected JPEG bytes, timestamps and acknowledged results while an upload or save is being completed or recovered. These local files support retries without treating an unknown outcome as a new action. They are separated by account and use iOS file protection and atomic writes.

Displayed content and authorized images can be held in memory; network services may use ordinary response caches. Signing out clears the active session and visible account state but is not a full erasure of all account-specific retry files. Unresolved local work may remain on the device. Deleting cloud data does not mean every device file or system cache has been erased.

Local archive reads content created by older device-only versions without automatically uploading or changing it. You must review and confirm each selected note or post before it is copied into your current cloud account. A copied post is a private draft; a photo requires its own confirmation. Original archive items remain on the device. Historical chats and wallet credits are not transferred.

The photo picker grants access to the items you select, not unrestricted access to the whole library. Apple may fetch an iCloud Photos item according to your settings. Friend-code scanning, where available, uses optional camera access to read a code on the device; camera frames are not recorded or uploaded by that scanner. In the current online app, unavailable call controls do not start a live call or capture microphone audio.

Toti does not request contacts, precise location, health-data or advertising-tracking access, and does not register for remote push notifications. Changing a device permission stops later access but does not delete copies already stored. Choosing a copy-diagnostics control can place the displayed diagnostic text on the system clipboard; account deletion does not clear a copied clipboard item.

4. Service providers, network information and sharing

4.1 Supabase hosting

Toti uses a dedicated Supabase project for guest authentication, database records, private media storage and server functions. Requests can expose your IP address, request path, timestamps, response/error codes, client/device software information and authentication or account context to the hosting infrastructure. Providers process operational and security logs to run and protect the service; Toti does not promise that guest requests are unidentifiable or unlogged.

The selected project region is Singapore (ap-southeast-1). Core project hosting does not guarantee that every request, log or support operation stays in Singapore: infrastructure, support and content-delivery processing may occur in other countries. Provider information is available at https://supabase.com/privacy and https://supabase.com/legal/privacy-resources/data-residency-and-transfers-faq.

4.2 Public agreements and app materials

The agreement reader downloads public PDFs from Toti's Supabase Storage. No login, API key or signed link is required to read them, but the hosting/CDN service receives ordinary request information, including IP address and requested file. Google Docs is not used. Opening a PDF does not send your private posts, notes, chats or wallet archive to the public agreement bucket.

Supplied artwork may be bundled with the app or fetched from public Toti asset URLs. A runtime asset request can reveal ordinary network information to its host; a bundled image displayed locally does not require a request. Public artwork and agreement buckets are separate from private uploaded media.

4.3 Apple, outside services and correspondence

Apple handles App Store distribution, device permissions, photo selection, any iCloud Photos retrieval and device backups according to its services and your settings. Historic Apple-billed transactions and system diagnostics remain subject to Apple's practices. See https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-store/.

If you open an external website or independently share content, the destination receives information under its own practices. If you email the contact address after it is supplied, the operator and its email provider receive your sender address, message and attachments. We use correspondence to handle the request, investigate related issues and meet legal duties. Do not send passwords, session tokens, payment-card details or unrelated private notes. The support provider and retention arrangements remain to be confirmed before publishing the address.

We do not sell personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising or use it for advertising tracking. The app has no integrated advertising, third-party usage-analytics or third-party crash-reporting SDK. This does not eliminate Supabase infrastructure logging or diagnostics that Apple handles under device settings. Toti does not send notes or messages to an AI provider.

Authorized personnel and service providers may access information as necessary for operation, actual media review, security, support or legal duties. We may disclose relevant information when required by law or valid legal process. These access purposes do not make private notes or pending images publicly visible to members.

5. Why information is processed

We process account and content information to provide the features you request, maintain access and visibility rules, display profiles and discussions, store private writing, manage invitations and membership, review submitted images, handle reports, recover interrupted actions and secure the service. Agreement records identify the policy version associated with your choice.

Where a legal basis is required, processing necessary for requested service functions relies on providing the service; proportionate security and abuse-prevention processing relies on legitimate interests where permitted; legal obligations apply to required records or disclosures; optional permissions and consent apply where required. You can decline an optional upload or permission and continue using functions that do not need it. This notice is not blanket consent to unrelated processing or future features.

6. Storage, retention and security

Active cloud account records and content generally remain until you remove them through a supported control or delete the account. Individual removals may hide or mark records as deleted while technical records remain. There is no universal automatic expiry for every post, message, report or note. Media revisions, operation receipts, safety records and deletion-job status can have a different lifecycle from the visible content.

Delete account starts cleanup of the account's active cloud records and owned media. Limited deletion status records, provider security logs and infrastructure backups may remain according to their purpose and retention controls. This version does not publish a verified fixed-day retention schedule for all provider logs or backups, and does not claim immediate erasure from every backup. Information subject to a legal retention requirement may need to be kept only for that purpose.

Local archives and unresolved retry data can remain in the app container until removed through an available control or by deleting the app and its data. Offloading can retain app documents. Device or computer backups may include app files according to your settings; manage those backups with the relevant provider. Logging out does not delete cloud information.

The service uses HTTPS and account-based access controls; private media is separated from public assets. Local session storage and protected retry files reduce exposure, but the app is not end-to-end encrypted against the service operator and does not provide a separate app passcode. An unlocked or compromised device can expose content. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

7. Your controls and account deletion

Use profile controls to change your name, biography or avatar, and available content controls to manage your posts, drafts, comments, notes, saved choices and relationships. You may decline photo selection, revoke camera access in iOS Settings, leave supported conversations or rooms, or stop using the service. The app does not currently provide a complete automated export of all account data.

To delete the current cloud guest account, open Me > Settings > Delete account while signed in and confirm. The service denies normal account access, removes owned profile/content and owned post, room or group containers with their associated records, removes owned uploaded media and deletes the authentication identity. Independently owned content of unrelated accounts is outside that request. Removing a shared container can make its replies/messages unavailable to its participants.

A pending or failed cleanup is not confirmed full deletion. The app may sign out while the service finishes cleanup; an interrupted deletion can require trusted administrative reconciliation. Keep any error details needed for a support request without sharing session credentials. Limited technical status, provider logs, backups and legal retention are described above.

Cloud deletion does not erase original Photos items, the earlier Local archive, every device retry file, independently shared copies, emails, clipboard content, Apple records or separately managed backups. Deleting one Notebook item does not delete a Lounge draft copied from it. If you also want to remove local app files, manage the app and its data through iOS, and manage backups separately.

Log out or uninstalling does not delete your guest cloud account and may remove the only session through which you can access it. Toti has no current email/password or account-linking recovery method for an unlinked guest. Delete the cloud account before signing out if that is your intention.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, erase or receive personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Use the contact email once completed. Identity verification must be proportionate; inability to verify ownership of an unlinked guest can limit what can safely be disclosed or changed. Do not send an identity document unless it is necessary and specifically requested.

8. Age requirement

Toti is intended for adults aged 18 or older, subject to any higher applicable minimum, and is not directed to children. It does not currently verify identity documents or dates of birth. If information about an underage person has been provided, use account/content deletion where available and contact the operator once the email address is completed. The age rule is not a claim that age is technically verified.
