Toti — Share your music world. Keep your inspiration.

A favorite song, a concert memory, a lyric waiting to happen. Toti brings music conversations and personal creativity together, with space to meet people, share your perspective, and collect ideas for whatever comes next.

DISCOVER YOUR MUSIC COMMUNITY
Explore Lounge posts about listening, concerts, making music, and finding a bandmate. Browse Toti editorial stories and community posts, visit author profiles, and explore the people and ideas behind each conversation. Search by text, topic, or author, and switch between For you, Following, and Saved.

SHARE WHAT MOVES YOU
Write a music post, choose a topic, and add an optional photo. Save unfinished thoughts as private drafts, then publish when you are ready. Like posts, leave replies, bookmark favorites, and share discoveries in your conversations. Ordinary comments and messages are free.

MAKE ROOM FOR MUSIC
Explore themed rooms spanning pop, soul, jazz, acoustic, classical, and electronic music. Browse Toti's illustrated room collection, or create and join member rooms for text conversations around shared interests. Room artwork and editorial characters are labeled separately from real members. Live room audio is not currently available.

CATCH YOUR NEXT IDEA
Keep lyrics, chord progressions, and songwriting thoughts in your private Notebook. Start with a blank page or an editable writing prompt, organize notes under Lyrics, Chords, or Ideas, and add a mood to capture the feeling. Search your writing, pin important notes, and edit, duplicate, or delete entries as your ideas develop.

TURN INSPIRATION INTO A POST
Ready to share something from your Notebook? Copy a note into a separate Lounge draft and shape it for the community. Your original note stays private, and you decide what to publish.

KEEP YOUR CONVERSATIONS TOGETHER
Find people, follow their profiles, and continue the conversation in Chats. Exchange text, photos, and files, create groups, and manage group invitations. Keep unfinished text as drafts and organize your inbox with search, pin, mute, and personal read controls.

OPEN VOICE AND VIDEO CALL SCREENS
Open dedicated voice and video call screens right from a conversation. Preview your camera, switch between front and rear cameras, and manage camera permissions from the video screen. Live voice and video connections are not currently available.

MAKE YOUR PROFILE YOUR OWN
Choose a profile photo, edit your name and introduction, and bring your published posts, liked posts, saved discoveries, and private drafts together in Me. Visit other profiles to explore their posts and follow the people whose music interests speak to you.

STAY IN CONTROL
Manage your own posts and replies, report content, and block accounts. Review your safety preferences in Settings, read the Terms and Privacy Policy through the shared PDF reader, and explore the credits behind Toti's photography and artwork. Uploaded profile and post photos require review before they become publicly visible.

YOUR MUSIC, YOUR SPACE
Keep personal writing private to your account and share community posts on your own terms. Move between conversations and creativity, with a place for both the thoughts you share and the ideas you are still finding words for.

Find something that moves you. Write something worth keeping. Make it Toti.
